Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Picopop is published by 2RK, 248 rue du Haou, 40230 Bénesse-Maremne, France (SIREN 106 224 702). We take privacy seriously — especially because Picopop is enjoyed by families and children. This policy explains what data is processed by this website and by the Picopop mobile application.

1. This website (picopop.app)

  • No user account, no contact form, no newsletter.
  • No cookies, no advertising, no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Our hosting provider (Vercel Inc.) may process technical request data (such as IP addresses) strictly for the purpose of serving and securing the website.

2. The Picopop application

  • No account. The app can be used without creating any account and without providing any name, email address or other identifying information.
  • Camera. The camera is used on your device to scan QR codes printed in our books and to photograph your colored drawings. Photos of drawings are uploaded to our secure backend (Google Firebase) solely to generate the animation, and are not used for any other purpose.
  • No advertising, no tracking. The app contains no ads, no third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell or share personal data.
  • Local storage. Downloaded books and preferences (such as language) are stored locally on the device so the app works offline.

3. Children's privacy

Picopop is designed to be used by children together with their parents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Scanned drawings are not linked to any identity. If you believe personal data concerning a child has been transmitted to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

4. Data retention and deletion

Scanned images are kept only as long as necessary to provide the animation service. You may request the deletion of any data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

5. Your rights

In accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, and the right to lodge a complaint with the French supervisory authority (CNIL, cnil.fr).

6. Contact

7. Changes
