How to test PicoPop without any printed material

PicoPop turns a child's colored‑in drawing into a short animated film. In normal use, an adult adds a physical PicoPop book, the child colors a page, and the adult scans it. For this review you don't need to print or color anything — use the test book and the pre‑colored image below.

✓ No account, no data collectedThe scanned photo never leaves the device. No sign‑up, no tracking, no ads.
General audienceNot “Made for Kids”. An adult operates the app; the child just watches the film.
Test content includedA private, unlisted test book you add with the QR code below — it never appears publicly.

Step by step

  1. Add the test book

    Open the app → Scan tab → Add a book → scan QR 1 below. “TEST BOOK (REVIEW)” is added to My Books on the Home tab.

    This book is published but unlisted — it never shows up publicly; this QR code is the only way to add it.

  2. Open the coloring

    From Home, open TEST BOOK (REVIEW) → tap TEST COLORING (REVIEW) – T REX to reach the scan screen.

    Shortcut: Scan tab → Scan a coloring page → scan QR 2 to jump straight to this scan screen.

  3. Import the pre‑colored image

    On the scan screen, tap Import a picture and choose the provided file TEST-COLORING-REVIEW-T-REX.png. The four corner markers are detected automatically.

    By design, importing a picture with no markers is refused — only a genuine PicoPop page can be scanned (a random photo can’t).

  4. Watch it come to life

    The colored T‑Rex animates into a short film. You can save it to the in‑app gallery and share it with the system share sheet.

Notes for the reviewer

Privacy. The coloring photo stays on the device — it is not uploaded or stored. For a few animations the image is sent over HTTPS to an image‑processing service that handles it in memory and does not keep it. Full policy: picopop.app/confidentialite.

Business model. PicoPop books are physical products; the app is free and animates the pages a child has colored. The test book above lets you experience the full flow without a physical book.

Camera permission. Used only to scan QR codes and photograph a coloring page; the video stream is never recorded. On this review path you can use image import instead of the camera.

Language. The app follows the device language automatically — it is in English on an English device. It can also be switched anytime under the ☰ menu → “Language”.
