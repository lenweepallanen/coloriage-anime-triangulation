## 🇺🇸 English (United States)

**App name** (≤30) : `PicoPop`

**Short description** (≤80) :
`Your child colors, you scan — the drawing becomes a short educational film.`

**Full description** (≤4000) :
```
Turn your child's coloring into a magical little film! ✨

With PicoPop, your child colors a page from their PicoPop coloring book however they imagine it. You scan it with your phone, and the drawing — in THEIR own colors — comes to life as a lively animated film to watch together and share with the whole family.

And it's more than a cute moment: every film is packed with fun facts and educational tidbits (animals, dinosaurs, nature and more) that spark curiosity — and inspire your child to color, draw and imagine again and again.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Add your PicoPop book by scanning its QR code.
2. Let your child color the page of their choice.
3. Scan the page: the colored drawing comes to life as an animation.
4. Save the film and share it with the people you love.

FUN & EDUCATIONAL
• Inspires kids to color, draw and get creative
• Little films full of fun facts and educational discoveries
• The magic of seeing their own drawing move, in their own colors
• A lovely moment to share as a family, again and again

PRIVACY FIRST
• No account, no sign-up
• No ads, no trackers
• Coloring photos stay on your device — never published or stored

GOOD TO KNOW
PicoPop works with PicoPop coloring books (a physical product). An adult scans and stages the drawing; the child watches their artwork come alive.

Color. Scan. It comes to life!
```
